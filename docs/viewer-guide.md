# RiftSight — Viewer Guide

You've been invited to help test RiftSight on a streamer's channel. Here's everything you need to know — it takes about a minute.

## What you need to do

1. Make sure the streamer (or whoever invited you) has confirmed your Twitch account is on the test list. If you haven't heard back that you're confirmed, hovering cards won't work yet — check with them first.
2. Open the streamer's channel on Twitch, like you normally would.
3. If a small disclosure/consent popup appears from Twitch about the extension, accept it.
4. Hover your mouse over any card visible on the stream. You should see its full art pop up.

**You don't need to install anything.** No browser extension, no download — just watching the stream in a normal browser is enough.

## One thing to know before you report something as broken

If you just opened the stream, or just refreshed the page, **give it a little time before hovering** — anywhere from a few seconds up to a few minutes, depending on how the streamer has their delay configured. During that window the overlay may show nothing at all, even though it's working correctly. This is expected, not a bug: RiftSight deliberately waits out the streamer's own configured delay before showing anything, rather than risk showing you a snapshot that doesn't match what's actually on screen. If it's been a while and cards still aren't appearing, that's worth reporting.

## Things worth trying

- Hover over different cards in different areas of the board.
- Try switching between normal, theater, and fullscreen view, if you can.
- Resize your browser window and see if things still line up.

## If something looks wrong

Let the streamer (or whoever invited you) know, with as much of this as you can:

- Which card you hovered
- What you expected to see vs. what actually happened
- Your browser (Chrome, Firefox, etc.) and roughly what size/mode you were watching in
- Roughly when it happened

Thanks for helping test!
