RiftSight Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

RiftSight is an unofficial, fan-made Twitch Extension that shows card details for Riftbound games streamed through RiftAtlas. RiftSight is not affiliated with Riot Games, UVS Games, RiftAtlas, or Twitch.

If you're a viewer

RiftSight does not collect, store, or process any personal data about viewers. When you watch a stream with RiftSight activated, your browser verifies your session with Twitch's own Extension platform to confirm which channel you're watching — this happens entirely through Twitch's standard Extension Helper mechanism and is never stored by RiftSight. We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind on the viewer-facing overlay.

If you're a streamer

To use RiftSight as a streamer, you connect your Twitch account through Twitch's own OAuth authorization flow. We store:

  • Your Twitch user ID and Twitch login name, so we know which channel to publish card data to.
  • A one-way cryptographic hash of a connection credential we issue you (never the credential itself), so we can verify future connections came from you.
  • Basic timestamps (when your account was linked, when a credential was last used or rotated).

We never store your Twitch password — that's handled entirely by Twitch's own OAuth consent screen and never passes through RiftSight. We do not sell or share this data with any third party.

What RiftSight publishes to your stream

While you're actively using RiftSight, it reads publicly visible card information from your own RiftAtlas browser tab (card art, names, and stats already visible on your screen) and relays it in real time so viewers can see it in the overlay. This never includes hidden information (opponents' hands, hidden zones) and is not retained after your stream ends.

Operational logs

RiftSight's backend keeps short-lived operational logs (connection counts, error rates, timestamps) to keep the service running reliably. These logs never include card data, viewer identities, or full credential values.

Removing your data

If you stop using RiftSight, you can ask us to remove your linked account and all associated data by emailing riftsight.support@gmail.com.
