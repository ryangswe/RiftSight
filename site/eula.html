RiftSight is an unofficial, fan-made Twitch Extension that shows card details for Riftbound games streamed through Rift Atlas. RiftSight is not affiliated with Riot Games, UVS Games, Rift Atlas, or Twitch. By using RiftSight — as a viewer or as a streamer — you agree to these terms.

What RiftSight does

For viewers: hovering over a card visible on a participating streamer's video shows that card's art, with no install or account required. For streamers: a browser extension reads publicly visible card information from your own Rift Atlas browser tab and relays it, through RiftSight's backend, to your Twitch Extension so viewers can see it. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what data that involves.
