How it works

One-time setup for streamers. None for viewers.

For streamers
  1. Install the browser extensionAdd RiftSight to Chrome and connect your Twitch account.
  2. Enable the RiftSight Twitch ExtensionAdd it to your channel and activate it as a video overlay.
  3. Open Rift Atlas and go liveYour visible cards sync to viewers automatically while you play.

Why RiftSight

Built for watching Riftbound

Read cards instantly

See the full card image without pausing or leaving the stream.

Stay in the action

No squinting, searching, or interrupting playback.

Almost no viewer setup

Viewers only need to activate the Twitch extension on the stream.

Works across Twitch modes

Normal, theater, and fullscreen — hover works the same everywhere.
